#+title: SNB trigger activity study
#+include: ../../docs/include-topic.org

The tech note in [[file:technote/][technote/]] (LaTeX, built with =make RUNS=...=)
supersedes the results here from version 2 on: HD and VD, the neighborhood
sum method, rare-source boosts and the per-tonne SNB reference.  This file
keeps the v1 record.

* Goal

Measure how well trigger activities (TAs) select supernova neutrino burst
(SNB) interactions against radiological backgrounds, and estimate the data
rate of reading out the detector with each TA as the seed of a block of
data: initially 200 µs in time over all channels of one HD APA (or VD CRU).

* The job

=trig/cfg/snb-ta-study.jsonnet= simulates one APA of the DUNE FD HD 1x2x6
geometry (both faces) in independent frames of one readout window
(=params.daq.nticks=, 6000 ticks of 500 ns):

#+begin_example
  LowEDepoSource ─▶ DepoSetFanout ─┬─▶ DepoSetDrifter ─▶ DepoTransform ─▶ Reframer
   (MARLEY SNB +                   │   ─▶ AddNoise ─▶ Digitizer ─▶ TPFinder ─▶ TAFinder ─┐
    radiologicals)                 │                                                     ▼
                                   └───────────── depos (truth) ──────────────────▶ TATruth ─▶ TensorFileSink
#+end_example

=trig/cfg/run-snb-ta-study.sh OUTDIR SEED NFRAMES [-A ...]= runs one job:
MARLEY, then =wire-cell=.  Jobs with different seeds run in parallel and
=trig/pytools/snb_ta_metrics.py= reads all their outputs.

** Kinematics

- SNB :: MARLEY 1.2.1 ν_e CC on ⁴⁰Ar (=ve40ArCC_Bhattacharya2009=), as a JSON
  file.  The spectrum is a pinched thermal ("beta-fit") of mean 11 MeV and
  pinching 2.5 by default.  Directions are isotropic.
- Radiologicals :: =trig/cfg/radiologicals.jsonnet=: ³⁹Ar (1.01 Bq/kg), ⁴²Ar
  and ⁴²K, ⁸⁵Kr, and ²²²Rn with its short-lived daughters in equilibrium,
  with simplified decay schemes.  Activities other than ³⁹Ar follow the
  order of magnitude of the DUNE radiological model and must be checked
  before results are quoted.  Neutrons, external gammas and surface activity
  are not included yet.

Interactions of each source are Poisson in number per frame, uniform in time
over the readout window plus the longest drift before it (so the charge
arriving during the readout is in steady state), and uniform in the APA's
sensitive volume.

** Boosted SNB rate

The SNB rate is set far above a physical one (=snb_rate_hz=, default
1000 Hz per APA, about 5 interactions per frame) to spend compute on
signal.  =snb_physical_rate_hz= (default 0.5 Hz per APA: about 3000 events in
40 kt over 10 s for a burst at 10 kpc, shared over 150 APAs per 10 kt)
records a reference.  Their ratio, the "boost", rescales SNB rates in the
analysis.  SNB interactions are rare enough even boosted that they seldom
overlap each other, so only their rate, not their TAs, depends on the boost.

** From particles to depos: a toy transport

Without Geant4, =ToyTransport= turns final-state particles into energy
deposits in infinite liquid argon: electron steps with a tabulated stopping
power, bremsstrahlung and multiple scattering; photons with Klein-Nishina
Compton, photoelectric and pair attenuation; ions deposited at once;
neutrons dropped.  The recombination model (=BoxRecombination=) gives the
charge.  The aim is the right topology for TPs (MeV gammas spreading energy
over tens of cm, electron track lengths), not precision.  Neutron captures
from SNB events are missing.  Depos from Geant4 (eg edep-sim) can replace
the source later, provided they carry the same truth ids.

** Trigger chain

- TP :: =TPFinder= per frame, pedestal at the waveform mode, no filter,
  thresholds 70, 70, 60 ADC on U, V, W as dunetrigger's HD SimpleThreshold.
- TA :: =TAFinder= per frame, the DAQ Supernova algorithm with time AND
  channel clustering (=both=true=), one core per plane, TPs stored.  SWIFT
  is the alternative (=ta_algorithm=).

** Truth

Each interaction has an id (=64 n + source=) carried by its depos, and a
zero-charge marker depo with its energy (the neutrino energy for SNB).
=TATruth= projects the undrifted depos to wires and arrival times (drift
speed, lifetime, no diffusion), adds a per-plane delay from the response
plane to the TP start, and matches them to the TPs of each TA within one
channel and 10 µs.  The delays were measured with the =offset_est= column:
59, 61.5 and 64 µs for U, V, W.  Outputs per frame: tables of TAs (with
matched charge, signal charge, dominant interaction), interactions and
TA-interaction links.

* Metrics

=snb_ta_metrics.py= (numpy only) for seed TAs on one plane (default
collection) scanned over lower cuts on TA =ninputs= and =adc_integral=:

- A TA is signal if SNB sources give at least half of its matched charge.
- An SNB interaction is visible if its charge reaches the collection plane
  inside the readout window (50 µs margins), found if it gives at least half
  of a selected TA's charge.  Efficiency = found / visible.
- Background TA rate as simulated; SNB TA rate divided by the boost.
- Purity = signal / all TA rate, as simulated and at the physical rate.
- Data rate: blocks of 200 µs centered on each selected TA, all 2560
  channels, merged where they overlap; the covered fraction of time times
  2560 × 2 MHz × 1.75 B (packed 14-bit), per APA and for 150 APAs.  During a
  burst SNB blocks add their (unboosted) coverage.

* Results

See the latest entry under "Runs" below.  In short (v1): a cut on TA
=adc_integral= alone removes ³⁹Ar and ⁸⁵Kr while keeping SNB efficiency high;
the surviving background is from the MeV-scale decays, whose modeling then
decides the data rate.

* Runs

** v1 (2026-11-04)

Correction (v2): the reference SNB rate of 0.5 Hz per APA assumed 150 APAs
per 10 kt, but the simulated APA has two full drift volumes, 138 t of
argon, so at 0.0075 Hz/t its rate is 1.03 Hz.  The boost was 970, not 2000,
and the physical SNB TA rates and purities below are low by about 2.

24 jobs × 100 frames (seeds 100–123), 2400 frames = 7.2 s of live time per
APA, defaults (Supernova TA with both=true, thresholds 70/70/60, noise),
SNB at 1000 Hz (boost 2000 over 0.5 Hz).  Output in the xero area,
=topics/snbp/work/runs/v1/= (=metrics-plane2.txt=, =.json=).  About 10
minutes on 24 cores.

- 12 614 SNB interactions, 6952 visible; 1.78 M ³⁹Ar, 0.2 M ⁸⁵Kr decays.
- Collection-plane TAs: 94 kHz of background with no cut, 83 kHz of it ³⁹Ar
  and 10 kHz ⁸⁵Kr.  A 200 µs block per TA then covers all the time: full
  readout, 8.96 GB/s per APA.
- SNB interactions make 4.4 signal collection TAs each (MeV gammas and
  broken electron tracks); median TA =adc_integral= 1950, 90% above 210.
  Background TAs: median 630, 99% below 1960.

At loose cuts every visible SNB interaction is found, at all neutrino
energies; with ninputs ≥ 3 the efficiency rises from 0.68 below 10 MeV to 0.94 above
25 MeV.

What survives the tight cuts is ²¹⁴Bi (β up to 3.27 MeV with gammas) and
⁴²K (β up to 3.5 MeV): at adc ≥ 8000, 58 and 21 of 79 TAs; at ≥ 12000, 2
and 3 of 5.  These come from the least certain inputs (the ²²²Rn activity
of 1 mBq/kg with its chain in equilibrium in the bulk, simplified decay
schemes, the toy transport), and the missing neutron captures (6.1 MeV
cascades) and external gammas would add to exactly this region.  So the
tight-cut background rates and data rates are lower bounds of the model,
not predictions.

* Limitations and next steps

- Background statistics: a cut with no surviving background TAs bounds the
  rate only to about 1/(live time).  Tight cuts need more frames, or
  dedicated background-only runs with the fast parts only.
- The toy transport, simplified decay schemes and the missing backgrounds
  (neutrons, external gammas, surfaces) all bear on the tail of the
  background that survives tight cuts, which is what matters.
- The minimal block size is to be studied; the analysis takes =--block-us=.
- Induction-plane TAs and coincidences between planes are not used yet.
- VD (a CRU of =dunevd10kt-1x6x6-3view30deg=, 500 ns top) is the next
  geometry.
